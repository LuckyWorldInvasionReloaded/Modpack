# Lucky World Invasion Reloaded 1.2.16

## Balance
- Chaos Lucky Block: infusion is now capped at +75 Luck. Fully infused, it handed out a top-tier drop almost every other break; at +75 it stays the best block to infuse without turning into a legendary dispenser. Its infusion ingredients were rescaled to match (1 Krampus Horn or 3 Seeds of Chaos = a full infusion), and the rare Krampus Horn drop now gives 3 horns instead of 6.
- Lucky blocks at their maximum infusion now show it: the tooltip reads "+75 (max)" in gold.

## Invasions
- No ambush: after entering a dimension (or logging in), invasions cannot start on you for about 25 seconds - no more instant Nether invasions the moment you step through a portal.

## HUD
- The Lucky Stats HUD editor gets a whole-HUD text size setting (each line keeps its own size on top), position labels on every line ("Custom" once you have moved one), and a more compact list that fits the default GUI scale.

## Quality of life
- The Luck line on lucky block tooltips now always displays in English, whatever the game language.
- The Fuze lucky block is now called "Fuze Lucky Block".

## Fixes
- Ender Dragon: fixed the dragon staying at its low base health (~600 HP instead of ~2000+) for a whole fight - it happened mostly on servers that had (re)started while the dragon was already damaged. It now always gets its full fight health on the first hit.
- Yummy Lucky Block: the "Mysterious Soup" drop could fail and give nothing at all (about 1 time in 3). It now always serves its soups.
- Pink Lucky Block: the "Epic Lucky Pickaxe" was actually an axe; it is now a real Efficiency-100 netherite pickaxe. It also no longer counts (or sounds) as a legendary drop - the "Epic Lucky Axe" remains the legendary of the set.
- Pink Lucky Block: a hostile swarm drop was mistakenly tagged as a lucky outcome - good luck made it MORE likely, and it wrongly pushed your Avg Luck % up. It now correctly counts as unlucky.
