# Lucky Tweaks 1.0.10

## Player Locator (multiplayer)
- New: a strip above the XP bar showing the direction of the other players around you. Each player is a coloured marker that fades with distance, with a small up/down arrow when they're well above or below you. Players outside your field of view aren't shown.
- Hold the player list key (Tab) to turn the markers into player heads and raise name plaques above the bar; the rest of the HUD shifts up to make room.
- Server-controlled and on by default. Shows nothing when you're alone — markers only appear when other players are around.

## World restart
- New "Delete & Restart" button on the hardcore death screen: it deletes your current world and immediately creates a fresh one (new random seed), without a trip through the main menu. Like the other death-screen buttons it stays greyed out for about a second after you die, then it's a single click.

## Compatibility
- Kitty Slippers (Artifacts) keep creepers away even with Enhanced AI installed. Enhanced AI's creeper swell and launch behaviours used to ignore the slippers and blow up the wearer anyway; a creeper near a slippers wearer now simply flees, while Enhanced AI stays fully active against everyone else.
