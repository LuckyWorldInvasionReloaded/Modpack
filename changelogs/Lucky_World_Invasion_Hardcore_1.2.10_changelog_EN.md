# Lucky World Invasion - Hardcore 1.2.10

## Forge
- Updated to Forge 47.4.20 (from 47.3.10). Fixes a startup crash with recent PureSuffering and unblocks the Enhanced AI update.

## Mods
- More than 50 mods updated to current 1.20.1 builds. Notable: Enhanced AI 2.5 to 3.3.7 (smarter mob behavior), Biomes O' Plenty 18 to 19 with TerraBlender (new worldgen, applies to new chunks only), Yakurum 1.2 to 1.5, plus PureSuffering, Curios, InsaneLib, ModernFix and Improved Mobs.
- Removed unused or superseded mods: Sinytra Connector, FastFurnace, FastWorkbench, FLIB and Redirector.

## Lucky Tools 1.1.1
- Fixed the mod list (Catalogue) breaking at Lucky Tools.
- Tool 3D models set to a GeckoLib-supported geometry version (no visual change).

## Lucky Tweaks 1.0.1
- In-game config screen, listing every lucky block with its own controls.
- Worldgen control: global spawn multiplier and per-dimension spawn rules.
- Disable specific lucky blocks.
- Lucky weapons fix: self-harming effects (TNT, lightning, lava, fire, cobweb) fade as the weapon's Luck rises.
- Run integrity seal on the Lucky Stats screen (flags creative, cheats, non-hardcore, a modified config, or an edited save).

## Balance and fixes
- Water Lucky Block: disabled golden carrot and golden apple infusion (too cheap a path to high Luck).
- Water Lucky Block: fixed an infusion recipe that referenced a Yakurum block renamed in 1.5.
