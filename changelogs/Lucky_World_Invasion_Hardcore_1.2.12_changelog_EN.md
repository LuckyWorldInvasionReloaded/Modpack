# Lucky World Invasion - Hardcore 1.2.12

## Invasions
- Fixed invasions starting too slow and feeling almost empty. Mobs spawn close and fast again, while still never spawning within 16 blocks of you.
- Fixed invasions ending after only 3 kills (a testing value slipped into 1.2.11). The world's first invasion now needs 20 kills, later ones 30.
- Invasion progress is now shown on a proper boss bar instead of a custom overlay, so it plays nicely with Jade and other HUD mods.
- Invasions now play a victory sound when you clear them.
- No invasions happen in the End until you have beaten the Ender Dragon.
- The end-of-invasion message is now green.

## Luck modifier (new)
- A personal luck modifier now shows in your stats HUD. It drops when you fail an invasion and recovers (toward zero) when you clear one. While it is negative, your Lucky Block drops are worse, down to a floor.
- Your equipped Lucky Ring also adds its luck to this same modifier.

## Loot and balance
- All content-mod recipes are disabled again. Their items only come from lucky blocks (a Yakurum update had let some crafts back in).
- Pandilla Totem (Water Lucky Block) nerfed to 2 uses instead of 5, and only one drops.
- Disabled the Yakurum "damp temple" ocean structure, which handed out unlimited totems, and removed the rare gear from the other ocean structure chests.

## World
- Removed the "Overgrown Greens" biome (Biomes O' Plenty). Applies to new chunks only.

## Interface
- The pack version is now shown at the bottom of the main menu.
- The stats HUD now hides while the F3 debug screen is open.
