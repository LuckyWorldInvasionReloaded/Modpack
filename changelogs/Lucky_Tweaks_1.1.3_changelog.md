# Lucky Tweaks 1.1.3

## Changes
- Legendary and cursed drops are announced in chat, naming the lucky block they came from. Only with two or more players connected, and switchable off (`announceRareDrops`).
- A legendary is announced right away, with the drumroll, so everyone waits out the reveal together. A curse is held back five seconds, in step with its counter, so chat does not give it away before you feel it.
