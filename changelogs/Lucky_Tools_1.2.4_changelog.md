# Lucky Tools 1.2.4

- Lucky Totem: the boost now guarantees a legendary drop; the curse forces 2 cursed drops, only on blocks that actually have one.
- Lucky Wand: gambling costs a third of a level instead of a full one, and the roll runs 0 to 100 (no more negative; a 0 shows red with the curse sound).
- Lucky Shield: the dome now also grants Resurrection, and its cooldown is 2 minutes instead of 3.
