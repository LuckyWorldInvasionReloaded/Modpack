# Lucky World Invasion - Hardcore 1.2.9

## New: the Yummy Lucky Block

A food-themed lucky block, to fix the hunger problem that spoiled a lot of runs and was the last thing still forcing you to play regular survival. 30 possible outcomes, from cursed to legendary.

## Luck on the Fuze lucky block

- Outcomes are weighted by rarity instead of being drawn with equal odds.
- It reacts to your luck (Ring, Wand).
- The Wand and Totem work on it.
- The axe drop (the care package) has been reworked to be clearer.
- Several bugs fixed (ghost knockbacks, first breaks).

## Balance

- **Chaos Lucky Block**: nerfed. It was far too strong compared to the other blocks.
- **Tools Lucky Block**: infusion improved. Two redstone blocks are enough to reach max luck (+50).

## New: fusion and JEI

- **Fusion**: combine several lucky blocks of the same type into one to add up their luck.
- **JEI**: lucky block infusion and fusion recipes show up in JEI.

## Mods

The pack now includes two new mods made by Laink, **Lucky Tweaks** and **Lucky Stats** (luck fixing, per-block luck caps, fusion, and the stats screen/HUD with an open API). Lucky Tools is now 1.1.0. Your existing stats and curses carry over automatically.
