# Lucky World Invasion - Hardcore 1.2.14

## Legendary & cursed drops
- Some lucky block draws are now legendary: a suspense effect plays on every legendary drop, and a counter tracks them.
- Cursed draws are the opposite, the worst outcomes, with a discreet counter (updated a few seconds later so it doesn't spoil the draw).

## Lucky Tools
- Lucky Totem: the boost now guarantees a legendary drop; the curse forces 2 cursed drops, only on blocks that actually have one.
- Lucky Wand: gambling costs a third of a level instead of a full one, and the roll runs 0 to 100 (no more negative; a 0 shows red with the curse sound).
- Lucky Shield: the dome now also grants Resurrection, and its cooldown is 2 minutes instead of 3.

## Lucky blocks
- Fuze blockling: now drops backpack upgrade modules; the totem boost lands a tier+3 reward (grappling hook, portal gun, jetpack, advanced module).
- Chaos Lucky Block: Born in Chaos mobs and new curse "gauntlet" events.
- Pink Lucky Block: time-changing drops re-enabled.
- Yakurum storage and decoration blocks are craftable again.
- Yummy Lucky Block: fewer golden apple drops.
- Enchanted golden apple infusion halved (Water, Yummy).
- Water Lucky Block: magic coral made rarer.
- Pandilla totem (Water Lucky Block): capped at 2 uses.
- Nether guardian statue: coral drop capped.
- Fixed broken nested drops on the Frost and Elemental blocks.

## Stats
- The legendary and cursed counters now track the actual marked drops instead of a probability estimate.
- Playtime now shows seconds.

## Multiplayer & fixes
- Added PlayerRevive, off by default (death stays hardcore); turn it on for co-op multiplayer.
- The crocodile no longer destroys the weapon it swallows: it drops it back when killed.
- Fixed an Enhanced AI "open doors" server crash and a Yakurum armor tooltip crash.
- New worlds no longer start at "day 20" (a Poseidon drop was resetting world time).
- Quick beds can no longer be placed in mid-air (flight exploit).
