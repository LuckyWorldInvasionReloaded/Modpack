# Lucky World Invasion Hardcore - Proposed 1.2.5 update

## New Lucky tools (3)

The **Tools Lucky Block** now drops **8 tools** (the original 5 plus the 3 below):

- **Lucky Hammer** — sacrifice 30 lucky blocks (break them while holding it: they drop nothing, that's the cost) to charge the hammer, then use it **once at an anvil** on a weapon or armor piece: full repair + true indestructibility (Unbreaking 100 + Unbreakable). Single use.
- **Lucky Ring** — a ring (Curios equipment). While worn, **every lucky block you break raises your general luck a little** (up to +30%, with diminishing returns: impactful early, never too strong after hundreds of blocks). Everything you break gets luckier; the ring shows its current %.
- **Lucky Belt** — a belt (Curios equipment, tinkerer style). While worn, it **doubles your Tools Lucky Block jackpot chance**.

The ring and belt are **Curios equipment**: worn at the same time in their dedicated slots (right-click to equip), and compatible with every other lucky tool. Curios is already provided by the modpack.
