# Lucky Tools 1.2.6

- The Lucky Totem is now the Lucky Idol. A totem is nearly always something that saves you from death, and this one never did, so the name promised the wrong thing.
- Nothing is lost: the Idols you already have become Lucky Idols on their own when the world loads, keeping their remaining uses.

## Language
- English and French, including the Lucky Wand stats on the Lucky Stats screen.
