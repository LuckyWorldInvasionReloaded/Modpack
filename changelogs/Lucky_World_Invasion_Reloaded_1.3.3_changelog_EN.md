# Lucky World Invasion Reloaded 1.3.3

## Fixes
- Blocks from a Lucky Event came back forever when broken by anything other than your hand: a hammer, a piston, an explosion. The block reappeared four times a second while you kept everything you picked up, which duplicated items endlessly.
- The XP bar works again with mods that animate it: its animations had stopped since Lucky XP replaced the vanilla bar.

## Changes
- Legendary and cursed drops are announced in chat, naming the lucky block they came from. The legendary announcement lands with the drumroll, so everyone waits out the reveal together; a curse stays quiet a few seconds so chat does not give it away.
- New mod: Immersive Hotbar. The blue Lucky XP bar mirrors its animations in blue and follows its settings.
- The two XP bars now read as one pair: both numbers centered, both present from spawn, both hidden in creative.
