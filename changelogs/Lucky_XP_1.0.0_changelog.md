# Lucky XP 1.0.0

First release. A second experience economy for the Lucky Block mod: earn Lucky XP, spend it at machines and a merchant.

## Lucky XP
- Breaking a lucky block gives Lucky XP, with its own blue bar and blue orbs. A legendary drop gives double.
- Lucky Experience Bottle: bursts into Lucky XP orbs where it lands.

## Lucky Labs
- Stands generate in the world: a vending machine and the merchant who tends it. The whole build is indestructible.
- Using one starts a 3 minute timer. When it runs out the lab breaks apart and the merchant blows up. Spend fast.

## Vending machines
- Four types: Consumables, Materials, Tools, Infused Lucky Blocks.
- Each line sells once and never restocks. Purchases drop on the ground in front of the machine.
- Prices scale with rarity. The Infused machine always holds a legendary Water or Chaos block.
- Prices sit on Lucky XP orbs, and go dark when you can't afford the line.

## The merchant
- Eight services: reroll stock, convert machine type, Luck boost, permanent Luck, full heal, repair, lower the team's difficulty, extra life.
- His rarity is his discount: Rare -10%, Epic -30%, Legendary -60%. He wears it, so you can read it before opening the shop.
- Animated, follows you with his eyes, cackles on every sale.

## Lucky Events
- A roulette rolls a prize, then lucky blocks appear around every player: either infused with Luck, or worth extra XP when broken.
- Best result is a Mega jackpot. One event also rolls on its own each morning.
- Event blocks can only be removed by breaking them.

## Lucky Glasses
- Worn in a Curios head slot. Shows the Luck of nearby lucky blocks. Sold at the Tools machine.

## Language
- English and French.
