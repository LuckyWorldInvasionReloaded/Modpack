# Optional Suffering 1.1.5

- Dimension entry grace: after entering a dimension or logging in, invasions cannot start there for a configurable delay (default 25 seconds) - the invasion is suppressed and its mobs cleared, with no luck malus and no effect on the first-invasion goal. No more being ambushed the instant you step through a portal. Works with every kind of teleport (portals, portal guns, modded teleports). Config: dimensionEntryGraceSeconds (0 disables), also editable in the in-game config screen.
